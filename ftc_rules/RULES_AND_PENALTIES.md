
# FTC BIOBUZZ — Rules and Penalties

**Season:** 2026–2027  
**Game:** BIOBUZZ  
**Official source:** [Competition Manual](https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/game/manual)

> This is a team reference, not a replacement for the manual. Check the latest manual, Team Updates, and official Q&A before relying on a rule interpretation.

## 1. Match Eligibility

- Teams must meet FIRST registration and competition eligibility requirements.
- A robot must pass the required initial inspection before participating in Qualification or Playoff Matches.
- At least one member of the Drive Team must attend each assigned Qualification Match, subject to the official rules.
- Robot changes during an event may require reinspection before the next match.
- The Lead Robot Inspector has final authority over robot construction eligibility.

## 2. Robot Compliance

The robot must comply with the current construction rules, including requirements for:

- Robot dimensions and expansion.
- Approved components and mechanical systems.
- Motors, actuators, electronics, and control systems.
- Batteries, wiring, and electrical safety.
- Robot assembly and component attachment.
- Operator Console and Driver Station setup.
- Safe interaction with people, the field, and other robots.

Do not rely on an old inspection result after making a modification. Check whether reinspection is required.

## 3. Match Conduct

Teams and robots must not:

- Deliberately eject scoring elements from the field.
- Damage or create significant mess in the arena.
- Operate the robot in a way that poses an undue hazard.
- Strategically force an opponent to violate a rule.
- Deliberately damage or functionally impair an opponent robot.
- Exceed the permitted robot expansion limits.
- Deliberately detach robot components where prohibited.
- Ignore instructions from event staff or referees.
- Use strategies that violate the Competition Integrity Contract or the intended gameplay.

Consult the exact game rule for exceptions, definitions, and penalties.

## 4. Penalty Reference

The manual defines the following consequences:

| Penalty | General consequence |
|---|---|
| Verbal Warning | Warning from event staff or the Head Referee |
| Minor Foul | 5 points credited to the opponent's match score |
| Major Foul | 20 points credited to the opponent's match score |
| Yellow Card | Serious warning for specified behavior or violations |
| Red Card | Disqualification from the match |
| Disabled | Robot outputs are deactivated for the remainder of the match |
| Disqualified | Match-point and ranking-point consequences apply as specified by the rules |

A second Yellow Card within the same tournament phase results in a Red Card.

### Examples of rule-specific consequences

- Deliberately ejecting a scoring element: Major Foul per element.
- Damaging the arena: Warning; more serious consequences apply if strategic or continued.
- Dangerous robot operation: Robot may be disabled, with additional penalties for subsequent violations.
- Strategic damage to an opponent robot: Major Foul and card penalties may apply.
- Exceeding expansion limits: Warning; a Major Foul may apply for strategic violations.
- Missing an assigned match: Disqualification from that match under the applicable rule.

These examples are summaries. Always check the precise rule and its stated penalty.

## 5. Serious Team and Event Violations

Event rules also cover eligibility, safety, behavior, and event procedures.

Serious or repeated violations may lead to escalation, match disqualification, award disqualification, or further action by FIRST, depending on the circumstances.

Teams should:

- Follow the Competition Integrity Contract.
- Treat students, volunteers, referees, and other teams respectfully.
- Avoid harassment, unsafe conduct, and deliberate circumvention of rules.
- Report concerns through the appropriate event process.
- Follow the Head Referee's gameplay rulings and the official appeal process, where applicable.

## 6. Before Every Match

- [ ] Robot is in an inspected, legal configuration.
- [ ] No uninspected modification has been made.
- [ ] Robot dimensions and expansion remain legal.
- [ ] All mechanisms are safe.
- [ ] Driver Station and Operator Console are compliant.
- [ ] Team members know the match plan and restrictions.
- [ ] Drive Team is present and ready.
- [ ] Robot is not likely to damage the arena or another robot.

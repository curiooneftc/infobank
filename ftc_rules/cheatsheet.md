
# FTC 2026–2027 BIOBUZZ — Match Cheat Sheet

**Official manual:** [BIOBUZZ Competition Manual — TU06](https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/game/manual)

## Match Clock

| Period | Duration | Key reminder |
|---|---:|---|
| AUTO | 0:30 | No driver input |
| Transition | 0:08 | No powered robot/mechanism movement |
| TELEOP | 2:00 | Drivers control the robot |
| FLOWER scoring unlocks | 1:00 remaining | Do not score in FLOWERS early |
| Final warning cue | 0:20 remaining | Prepare to finish safely |

## Scoring

| Action | Points |
|---|---:|
| LEAVE perimeter wall in AUTO | 3 |
| PARK in Loading Zone in AUTO | 5 |
| PARK in Loading Zone in TELEOP | 5 |
| HIVE TIP | 20 |
| POLLEN/NECTAR in upward-facing CELL at match end | 2 each |
| POLLEN/NECTAR in an owned FLOWER | 2 each |
| Bottom NECTAR bonus in a FLOWER | 5 |
| POLLEN/NECTAR in your alliance's GARDEN | 1 each |
| Win a qualification match | 3 RP |
| Tie a qualification match | 1 RP |

**Ranking points (RP):**
- **SWARM RP:** Combined LEAVE + PARK points meet the event threshold.
- **POLLINATOR 1 RP:** Reach the event's HIVE TIP threshold.
- **POLLINATOR 2 RP:** Reach the higher HIVE TIP threshold.
- For most non-championship events, the manual lists thresholds of **16 SWARM points, 4 TIPS, and 7 TIPS**, respectively. Check event-specific updates.

## Three Key Scoring Rules

1. **HIVE:** Launching into the upward-facing CELL is the only permitted way to earn a HIVE TIP.
2. **FLOWER:** POLLEN/NECTAR must be at least partially inside the scoring volume between the top and middle rings. Scoring must begin with one minute remaining; earlier scoring may still count but is subject to penalties.
3. **FLOWER ownership:** The alliance with the highest qualifying NECTAR of its color owns the FLOWER and scores its qualifying elements. The alliance with the lowest qualifying NECTAR of its color earns the bottom NECTAR bonus.

**GARDEN:** POLLEN/NECTAR must be at least partially inside the GARDEN zone. Either alliance may remove elements from either GARDEN.

## Match-Day Checklist

- [ ] Robot and mechanisms have passed inspection.
- [ ] Robot is correctly positioned and configured at the start.
- [ ] Preloads and scoring elements are correctly placed.
- [ ] Driver Station is connected and the correct OpMode is selected.
- [ ] AUTO runs without driver input.
- [ ] No powered movement during the 8-second transition.
- [ ] Do not start FLOWER scoring before the 1:00 mark.
- [ ] Make scoring actions clear and unambiguous to referees.
- [ ] Stop all powered movement at 0:00.
- [ ] Wait for the referee's signal before retrieving the robot.

## Penalties to Remember

- **Minor foul:** 5 points credited to the opponent.
- Major fouls, yellow cards, and red cards may apply for more serious or repeated violations.
- Do not deliberately hoard scoring elements to prevent opponent access.
- Do not damage or improperly manipulate the HIVE, FLOWER, field, or another robot.
- Follow referee instructions and compete in the spirit of the rules.
